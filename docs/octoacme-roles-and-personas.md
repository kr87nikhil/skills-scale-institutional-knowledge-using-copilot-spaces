# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works with QA/Testing Lead to ensure testability and acceptance criteria alignment
- Collaborates with Technical Lead/Architect on design and implementation approach
- Receives prioritization from Product Managers and Product Lead
- Updates Project Manager on progress and blockers

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Escalates strategic decisions and conflicts to Product Lead
- Collaborates with QA/Testing Lead on quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Works with Technical Lead/Architect on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Coordinates with Project Manager on timeline and resource planning

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works closely with Delivery/Scrum Master on day-to-day execution and blocker removal
- Escalates risks to Product Lead for governance-level decisions
- Coordinates with Developers, Technical Lead, and QA on dependencies and milestones
- Facilitates communication between Product Managers and the delivery team

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy and ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They collaborate with developers and product teams to define testability requirements and validate deliverables.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and test strategy for each project
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute or coordinate manual and automated testing
- Track quality metrics and defect trends
- Validate that Definition of Done includes testability requirements
- Identify quality risks and blockers

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Reduce time spent on regression and rework
- Provide clear quality visibility to project teams

### Typical Communication
- Participates in sprint planning and acceptance criteria definition
- Provides quality status in weekly syncs
- Raises quality risks in risk registers
- Collaborates with Developers on test design and execution
- Reports quality metrics to Product Managers and Project Manager

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works with Developers to define testability requirements early in implementation
- Collaborates with Product Managers to refine and validate acceptance criteria
- Coordinates with Technical Lead/Architect on test infrastructure and automation
- Reports quality metrics and risks to Project Manager and escalates blockers

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads drive architectural decisions, establish technical standards, and mitigate technical risks. They work with developers and product managers to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical architecture and propose implementation approaches
- Review technical designs and PRs for alignment with standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers and support technical problem-solving
- Advocate for technical debt management and quality practices

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical risk and rework
- Build team capability and technical culture

### Typical Communication
- Leads technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Participates in sprint planning and risk assessment
- Mentors through code reviews and design feedback
- Participates in risk register discussions for technical risks

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partners with Developers to design and review technical solutions
- Collaborates with Product Managers and Product Lead on feasibility and trade-offs
- Works with QA/Testing Lead on test infrastructure and automation strategy
- Advises Project Manager on technical risks and dependencies
- Supports Delivery/Scrum Master in identifying and resolving technical blockers

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide executive-level product governance, oversee cross-project prioritization, and manage high-level stakeholder relationships. They set the product vision and ensure projects align with strategic business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Own product vision and long-term roadmap strategy
- Approve project charters and prioritization decisions
- Escalate and resolve cross-project dependencies and conflicts
- Review project outcomes against strategic metrics
- Manage executive stakeholder communications
- Support product managers in priority trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure product strategy alignment across projects
- Maximize business impact and customer value
- Maintain stakeholder confidence through transparent communication

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder reviews and executive updates
- Decision gates at project initiation and major milestones
- Weekly alignment with Product Managers and engineering leadership
- Executive steering committee participation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Receives project status and escalations from Product Managers and Project Managers
- Makes prioritization decisions and resolves cross-project conflicts
- Partners with technical and delivery leadership on feasibility and risk
- Communicates project outcomes and strategic alignment to executive stakeholders

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security and regulatory requirements. They work with engineering and product teams to embed security practices into the development lifecycle and manage incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Review designs and code for security vulnerabilities
- Manage security scanning and penetration testing in CI/CD
- Lead security incident response coordination
- Track compliance status and audit requirements
- Provide security guidance and training to teams

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and compliance violations
- Maintain customer trust and regulatory standing
- Embed security into development culture

### Typical Communication
- Participates in design reviews for security implications
- Reviews release checklists and deployment plans
- Leads incident response coordination and post-mortems
- Provides security training and best practice guidance

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partners with Developers on secure coding practices and vulnerability remediation
- Collaborates with Technical Lead/Architect on security architecture and infrastructure
- Works with Product Managers on security requirements and compliance trade-offs
- Coordinates with QA/Testing Lead on security testing and penetration testing
- Advises Project Manager on security risks and compliance blockers
- Supports release process with Security/Compliance Officer checkpoints

---

## Delivery/Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Delivery/Scrum Masters facilitate project ceremonies, remove team blockers, and ensure the team adheres to agreed processes and practices. They enable smooth execution and help maintain team velocity and morale.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives
- Track and escalate blockers and dependencies
- Maintain project board and iteration health
- Coach team on process adherence and continuous improvement
- Remove impediments to team progress
- Support team communication and transparency

### Goals
- Enable consistent, predictable team delivery
- Reduce blockers and dependencies that slow progress
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Leads daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Raises blocker escalations in daily syncs
- Tracks and reports on team velocity and health metrics
- Facilitates retrospectives and process improvements

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works closely with Project Manager on schedule and resource coordination
- Identifies and escalates blockers from Developers, QA/Testing Lead, and Technical Lead
- Facilitates communication between Product Managers and the delivery team
- Coaches the team on Agile practices and process adherence
- Supports retrospectives and continuous improvement initiatives
